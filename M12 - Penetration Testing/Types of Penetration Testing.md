**Types of Penetration Testing Assessments:**

1. **Goal-Oriented Pen Testing:**
   - **Definition:** Driven by specific, well-defined objectives set by the customer and pen tester.
   - **Purpose:** To achieve or assess the attainment of these goals through testing.
   - **Examples of Goals:** 
     - Gain remote access to an internal network.
     - Obtain domain admin access.
     - Access credit card information.
     - Create a denial-of-service attack or deface a website.
   - **Characteristics:** Focused and narrow, sometimes specific to areas like websites hosted externally.

2. **Compliance-Oriented Pen Testing:**
   - **Definition:** Driven by regulatory compliance requirements.
   - **Purpose:** To ensure adherence to compliance standards and frameworks (e.g., HIPAA, PCI-DSS).
   - **Examples:**
     - PCI-DSS assessment to protect credit card data.
   - **Characteristics:** Broad, addressing a range of protections and requirements mandated by laws or standards.

3. **Red Team-Based Pen Testing:**
   - **Definition:** Involves an internal red team simulating real-world attacker behavior.
   - **Purpose:** To evaluate overall security through adversarial techniques.
   - **Process:**
     - **Initial Assessment:** External network pen testing and open-source intelligence (OSINT) gathering.
     - **Execution:** Implement various attack vectors, including social engineering campaigns, physical infiltration attempts (e.g., dressing as janitorial staff), and internal network scanning.
   - **Team Dynamics:** 
     - **Red Team:** Acts as the attacker, mimicking real-world threat actors.
     - **Blue Team:** Focuses on defending the organization.
     - **Purple Team:** Can consist of members from both red and blue teams to enhance collaboration and effectiveness.
   - **Outcome:** A comprehensive report detailing methods used, findings, and recommendations. This type of testing is extensive and can be costly.

**Summary of Pen Testing Approaches:**
- **Goal-Oriented:** Focused on achieving specific objectives.
- **Compliance-Oriented:** Ensures adherence to regulatory standards.
- **Red Team-Based:** Simulates real-world attacks to assess overall security.

Penetration testing involves methodical approaches and various strategies to evaluate and improve organizational security.
