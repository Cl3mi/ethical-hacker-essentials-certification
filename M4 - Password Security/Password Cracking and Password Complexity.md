
1. **Password Cracking Techniques**: 
   - The objective is to discover and use a user's password to gain unauthorized access.
   - Weak or easily guessable passwords are targeted.
   - Password complexity is crucial: passwords should include letters, special characters, and numbers to increase difficulty.
   - Passwords that lack complexity (e.g., only numbers or letters) are easier to crack.
   
2. **Password Entropy**:
   - Refers to the measure of password strength irrespective of complexity.
   - Short passwords have low entropy, making them easier to crack even with complexity.
   - Passphrases, though less complex, can have higher entropy and are harder to crack due to length.

3. **XKCD Comic on Password Entropy**:
   - The XKCD comic illustrates the concept of password strength and entropy.
   - Highlights the importance of length in passwords (passphrases) over complexity alone.
   - Advocates for longer, less predictable passwords for better security.

These points emphasize the importance of both complexity and entropy in creating secure passwords to mitigate the risk of unauthorized access through password cracking techniques.
