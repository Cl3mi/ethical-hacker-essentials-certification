
**Definition of Hacking:**
- Hacking is using a complex system in a way it was not intended to be used, often by exploiting vulnerabilities.

**Purpose of Hacking in IT:**
- Hackers exploit vulnerabilities in complex IT systems to gain unauthorized access or achieve other goals, often leading to business loss or intellectual property theft.

**Who is a Hacker?**
- Someone with excellent computer skills, which can be learned.
- Hackers can be hobbyists, knowledge seekers, or have malicious intents such as stealing data or causing damage.

**Classes of Hackers:**
- **White Hat Hackers:** Ethical hackers who have permission to test and secure systems. They work as security consultants or pen testers.
- **Black Hat Hackers:** Malicious hackers who break into systems for illegal purposes without permission. They are criminals.
- **Gray Hat Hackers:** Individuals who might engage in both legal and illegal hacking activities. Their actions can be ambiguous but any illegal activity makes them criminals.
- **Suicide Hackers:** Black hat hackers who do not care about getting caught.
- **Script Kiddies:** Inexperienced hackers who use pre-written scripts and tools developed by others. They lack deep technical skills.

**Other Threat Actors:**
- **Cyber Terrorists:** Hackers with political or religious motives aiming to terrorize.
- **State-Sponsored Hackers:** Government-backed hackers engaged in cyber warfare.
- **Hacktivists:** Individuals or groups promoting political agendas through hacking.
- **Hacker Teams:** Organized groups working together like corporations to execute large-scale attacks.
- **Industrial Spies:** Corporate hackers spying on competitors.
- **Insiders:** Employees or trusted individuals who exploit their access to attack the organization.
- **Criminal Syndicates:** Organized crime groups engaging in large-scale cyber attacks for financial gain using sophisticated methods and resources like botnets.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Not all hacking is bad; ethical hacking (white hat) helps secure systems.
- Permission is the critical difference between ethical and unethical hacking.
- Various types of hackers and threat actors exist, each with different motives and methods.
