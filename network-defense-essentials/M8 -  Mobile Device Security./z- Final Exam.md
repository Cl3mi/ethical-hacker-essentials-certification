Here are the answers to the questions along with brief explanations:

### Question 1

**Which of the following mobile device connection methods enables secure communication between two mobile devices without data encryption as the devices in this method are connected through fixed paths without the interference of other devices?**

- **Point-to-point connection**

**Explanation**: A point-to-point connection enables direct communication between two devices over a fixed path, which can be secure due to the lack of interference from other devices. However, this method does not inherently include data encryption.

### Question 2

**Bob, an employee at an organization, lost his mobile phone that he was using for both personal and company purposes. After informing, the IT administrator sent a command to the lost mobile device that erased all the data stored in the Bob’s device.**

**Which of the following techniques is implemented by the IT administrator in the above scenario?**

- **Remote wipe**

**Explanation**: Remote wipe is a technique used to erase all data from a lost or stolen device remotely to prevent unauthorized access to sensitive information.

### Question 3

**Which of the following policies allows employees to use and manage the devices purchased by the organization and reduces the risks associated with BYOD by implementing stringent policies and protecting devices?**

- WRONG: **COBO (Corporate-Owned, Business-Only)**

**Explanation**: COBO policy allows employees to use devices purchased by the organization but restricts their use to business purposes only, minimizing risks associated with personal use.

### Question 4

**Axel, an employee of an organization, is using his personal mobile device to access the organizational resources. He was unsatisfied with the management, so he decided to sell the confidential corporate data stored on his device to the competitors.**

**Identify the risk associated with BYOD in the above scenario.**

- **Disgruntled employees**

**Explanation**: The risk in this scenario is related to disgruntled employees who may misuse their access to confidential corporate data, such as selling it to competitors.

### Question 5

**Which of the following practices helps network defenders in protecting mobile devices when they are connecting to a wireless network?**

- **Isolate a group of users using different SSIDs**

**Explanation**: Isolating users on different SSIDs (Service Set Identifiers) can enhance security by segmenting network traffic and reducing the risk of unauthorized access or attacks across different user groups.
