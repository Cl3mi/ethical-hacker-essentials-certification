## Summary: Security Awareness Training and Policies

### Importance of Security Awareness Training

- **Need for Awareness**
  - Many employees focus on their daily tasks and personal lives, often unaware of broader security threats.
  - It is crucial to inform employees about security risks and best practices through awareness training.

- **Types of Security Awareness Training**
  - **Periodic Training**: Conduct training sessions quarterly or regularly through various channels such as emails, HR websites, and seminars.
  - **Training Methods**:
    - Classroom style
    - Online courses
    - Roundtable discussions
    - Short films and creative content
    - Seminars and workshops

### Addressing Specific Security Risks

1. **Bluetooth Security**
   - **Risk**: Bluetooth malware can spread across devices within range.
   - **Training Focus**: Inform employees about risks associated with Bluetooth devices and enforce policies regarding their use on-premises.
