## Summary: Key Elements and Content of Security Policies

### Key Elements of Security Policies
- **Clear Communication**
  - Policies should be **brief** and **clear**.
  - Avoid verbosity; ensure that information is **informative** and easy to understand.

- **Scope Designation**
  - Define the **scope** of each policy clearly.
  - Ensure that policies are **enforceable by law**.
  - If an in-house legal team is unavailable, consider hiring a legal professional or service to review policies.

- **Responsibility and Guidance**
  - Recognize and outline **areas of responsibility**.
  - Provide **guidance** on implementation and compliance.

### Contents of Security Policies
- **High-Level Overview**
  - Include an overview of various security disciplines such as:
    - **Discipline Security**
    - **Safeguard Security**
    - **Procedural Security**
    - **Assurance Security**

- **Policy Descriptions**
  - Describe each policy based on its **requirements**.
  - Include details on **safeguards** and **procedures**.
  - Address aspects like **continuity of operations**.

- **Security Concept of Operations**
  - Define **roles**, **responsibilities**, and **functions** for security management.
  - Allocate **security enforcement** to **architectural elements**.
    - Example: Provide security measures for each **computer architecture** within the program.

### Typical Policy Document Content
- **Document Control**
  - Details on **document location**, **history**, **approvals**, and **distribution**.

- **Contact Information**
  - Include relevant **contact details** for policy-related inquiries.

- **Sanctions and Violations**
  - Outline potential **sanctions** and **violations** for non-compliance.

- **Glossary of Terms**
  - Provide a **glossary** of terms and **acronyms** used in the policy.
  - Ensure understanding of **three-letter acronyms (TLAs)** and **four-letter acronyms (SLAs)**.

### Summary
- **Enforcement and Compliance**
  - Ensure policies are **globally enforced**.
  - Include all necessary components to make policies **clear**, **enforceable**, and **comprehensive**.
