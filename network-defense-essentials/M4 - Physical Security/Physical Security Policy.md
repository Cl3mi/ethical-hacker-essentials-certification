### Importance of Physical Security Policy
- **Objective:** Ensure protection of the organization and its employees.
- **Guidelines:** Establish a policy to implement adequate security measures.

### Building Design and Regular Inspection
- **Regular Inspections:**
  - **Building and Parking Lot:** 
    - Check for maintenance issues such as faded stripes, potholes, or trip hazards.
    - Conduct both daytime and nighttime inspections.
  - **Internal Inspection:**
    - Look for physical security issues like doors held open by air pressure.
    - Inspect for hazards that may not be reported.

### Access Control for Visitors
- **Visitor Management:**
  - **Identification:**
    - Ensure that all visitors, including contractors and vendors, are identified before access is granted.
    - Implement a sign-in process and issue temporary badges if necessary.
  - **Entry Points:**
    - Confirm that all entry points are secured and properly managed.
    - For example, a previously observed school lacked positive control, allowing easy access to unauthorized individuals.

### Security Measures and Controls
- **Security Features:**
  - **Badges, Locks, and Keys:** 
    - Ensure that these security measures are in place and functioning.
    - Regular audits should be conducted to verify their effectiveness.
  - **Authentication Controls:**
    - Implement and maintain robust authentication measures.

### Video Surveillance
- **Surveillance Systems:**
  - **Monitoring:**
    - Verify that video surveillance footage is monitored regularly.
    - Ensure that someone is actively reviewing the footage.

### Asset Inventory Management
- **Inventory Techniques:**
  - **RFID and Stickers:**
    - Use RFID tags and stickers to facilitate quick and accurate asset tracking.
  - **Audits:**
    - Maintain a proper and updated inventory of assets.

### Summary
- **Physical Security Policy:** Essential for protecting the organization and employees.
- **Inspection:** Regular checks of the building and parking lot to identify and address issues.
- **Visitor Access:** Implement procedures for visitor identification and control.
- **Security Controls:** Ensure badges, locks, and authentication are functional and audited.
- **Video Surveillance:** Regularly monitor and review surveillance footage.
- **Asset Management:** Use RFID and stickers for efficient inventory tracking and auditing.
