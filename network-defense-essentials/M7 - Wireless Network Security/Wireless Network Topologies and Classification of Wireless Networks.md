## Wireless Network Topologies

- **Peer-to-Peer Communication**
  - **Ad-Hoc (IBSS) Mode:**
    - Enables direct communication between devices, like two laptops, without the need for a central access point.
    - Devices must be configured with the same channel and SSID.
    - Less commonly used; can be exploited in attacks to allow unauthorized access.

- **Basic Service Set (BSS)**
  - **Centralized Architecture:**
    - Involves an access point (AP) connecting to a switch, which in turn connects to the network (internet/intranet).
    - Common in small, medium, and large organizations.
    - Used to extend a wired network, e.g., adding an AP to extend coverage in a building.

- **Expanding Networks**
  - **Adding Access Points:**
    - Used to enhance coverage in large areas like multi-story buildings or large office spaces.
  - **LAN-to-LAN Wireless:**
    - Connects different LANs, potentially across adjacent buildings, using Wi-Fi.

## Wireless Network Classifications

- **Wireless Local Area Network (WLAN)**
  - **Description:**
    - Connects devices within a local area (home or office) wirelessly.
    - Extends from a single room to an entire campus.
    - The WLAN is accessed through an access point connected to a switch port.

- **Wireless Personal Area Network (WPAN)**
  - **Description:**
    - Covers a small area around an individual.
    - Examples include Bluetooth connections between devices like phones and headsets.
    - Known as a personal area network when connected through Bluetooth.

- **Wireless Wide Area Network (WWAN)**
  - **Description:**
    - Covers a large area such as regions, nations, or globally.
    - Example: **Starlink**
      - A constellation of satellites providing global internet access.
      - Currently around 1,200 satellites in low Earth orbit.
      - Plans for expansion to include more satellites and future deployments around Mars.

- **Wireless Metropolitan Area Network (WMAN)**
  - **Description:**
    - Covers a metropolitan area.
    - Utilizes exterior antennas and typically uses the 802.16 protocol.
    - Ideal for broader city coverage when direct connections are not possible.


## Exam
**Question 1**

**Which of the following types of network allowed Alice to view her daily health report on her mobile and watch in the above scenario?**

- **WPAN (Wireless Personal Area Network)**

**Explanation:** Alice's smartwatch and mobile phone are connected via Bluetooth, which is a technology used in WPANs. WPANs are designed to cover short distances, typically around an individual, which is suitable for connecting devices like a smartwatch and a mobile app.

**Question 2**

**Which of the following types of wireless network handles cellular network technology such as CDMA, GSM, GPRS, and CDPD for data transmission and has a built-in cellular radio that helps users send or receive data?**

- **WWAN (Wireless Wide Area Network)**

**Explanation:** WWANs are designed to cover large areas, including regional, national, or global scales, and handle cellular technologies like CDMA, GSM, GPRS, and CDPD. They are used for mobile communication and data transmission over broad distances.
