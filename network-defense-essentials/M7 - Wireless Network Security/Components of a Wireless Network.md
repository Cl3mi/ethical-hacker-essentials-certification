### Key Components of a Wireless Network

- **Access Point (AP)**
  - **Function:** Connects wireless devices (phones, laptops) to the network.
  - **Connection:** Uses Cat5e or Cat6 cables to connect to a switch, which then links to the network infrastructure.

- **Network Interface Card (NIC)**
  - **Function:** Allows devices to connect to the wireless network.
  - **Note:** "NIC card" is redundant; "NIC" is sufficient.

- **Wireless Modem**
  - **Function:** Processes the wireless signal, eliminating the need for physical cabling.

- **Wireless Bridges**
  - **Function:** Operate at the MAC layer to connect physically separate networks, expanding the coverage of a wireless network.

- **Repeaters**
  - **Function:** Amplify and retransmit weak signals to extend coverage.

- **Wireless Routers**
  - **Function:** Move traffic between different networks wirelessly.
  - **Note:** Similar to traditional routers but operate wirelessly.

- **Wireless Gateways**
  - **Function:** Route data packets and offer additional functionalities such as internet connection sharing.

- **Wireless USB Adapters**
  - **Function:** Allow devices without built-in wireless capabilities to connect to wireless networks via USB.
  - **Use Case:** Useful when internal wireless adapters fail or are outdated.

### Antennas

- **General Purpose of Antennas**
  - **Function:** Convert radio waves to electrical signals and vice versa for communication.

- **Transceiver**
  - **Function:** Combines transmission and reception through antennas.

- **Directional Antennas**
  - **Function:** Focus signal in a specific direction to minimize leakage.
  - **Example:** Useful for communication between buildings.

- **Parabolic Grid Antenna / Reflector Antenna**
  - **Function:** Focuses weak signals using a parabolic dish to enhance reception.
  - **Range:** Can pick up signals from distances up to 16 km or more.
  - **Note:** Dish is not the antenna; the antenna is positioned at the center of the dish.

- **Yagi Antennas**
  - **Function:** UHF antennas often used for TV reception in the past.
  - **Appearance:** Characterized by a central long bar with tapered elements on either end.

- **Other Antennas**
  - **Yagi Antennas:** Still useful for WIFI; they consist of a series of elements to focus signals.
  - **Reflector Antennas:** Include both parabolic and grid types, suitable for satellite communications and long-range reception.
