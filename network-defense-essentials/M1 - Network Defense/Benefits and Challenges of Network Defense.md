## Benefits of Network Defense

- **Protecting Assets**:
  - Assets are considered valuable (spelled as dollar signs).
  - Importance is measured in financial terms.
  - Protection is crucial for maintaining asset value.

- **Compliance with Regulations**:
  - Ensures adherence to industry and government regulations.
  - Examples:
    - **HIPAA**: For medical-related organizations.
    - **PCI DSS**: For organizations handling customer credit cards.

- **Ensuring Secure Communication**:
  - Maintains secure interactions with clients and suppliers.

- **Reducing Attack Risk**:
  - Minimizes the risk of cyberattacks.

- **Gaining Competitive Edge**:
  - Enhanced security can attract more customers compared to less secure competitors.
  - Secure services may lead to increased annual revenue.

## Challenges in Network Defense

- **Distributed Computing Environment**:
  - Complexity due to diverse services (e.g., Cloud services, virtual desktops, containerized applications).
  - Organizations may have complex, multi-layered environments.
  - Security professionals must adapt to the specific complexities of each organization.

- **Emerging Threats**:
  - New and sophisticated threats are continually evolving.
  - Attackers are often organized crime groups with advanced techniques.
  - Threats can include innovative methods to compromise and damage networks.

- **Lack of Network Security Skills**:
  - Many organizations struggle due to insufficient security expertise.
  - Continuous education and skill development are essential.
  - All levels of an organization, from executives to support staff, need to be aware of current threats and security practices.
