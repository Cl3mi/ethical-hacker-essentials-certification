## 1. Preventative
- **Description**: Methods used to avoid threats entirely.
- **Goal**: Ensure that attacks never happen.
- **Challenges**: Harder to measure effectiveness.
  
## 2. Reactive
- **Description**: Methods used to detect attacks that have already occurred.
- **Goal**: Identify and address attacks after they have happened.
- **Focus**: Speed of reaction is crucial.
- **Details**: 
  - Detection mechanisms are essential.
  - Quick response can mitigate damage.

## 3. Retrospective
- **Description**: Analyzing and addressing attacks after they have occurred.
- **Steps**:
  - **Remediation**: Identify and fix the causes of the attack.
  - **Eradication**: Remove the threat from the system.
  - **Recovery**: Restore affected systems and data.
- **Goal**: Learn from past incidents to improve defenses.

## 4. Proactive
- **Description**: Making decisions to prevent future attacks.
- **Goal**: Anticipate and defend against emerging threats before they occur.
- **Approach**: 
  - **Vision**: Look into the future and stay ahead of potential threats.
  - **Preparation**: Implement measures based on predicted threats.
- **Opinion**: Considered an ideal approach due to its forward-thinking nature.
