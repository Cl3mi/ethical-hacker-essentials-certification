- **Terminology and Acronyms**:
  - **GSM**: A global standard for mobile networks, commonly used internationally.
  - **Bandwidth**: Refers to the amount of data throughput on a network.
  - **Access Point**: Connects wireless devices to a network or the Internet.
  - **BSSID**: The MAC address of an access point.
  - **ISM**: Frequencies reserved for industrial, scientific, and medical use, shared by various devices.
  - **Hotspot**: A feature that allows devices to provide wireless Internet access.

- **Wireless Connection Concepts**:
  - **Association**: A process where a wireless device connects to an access point through a handshake.
  - **SSID**: A 32-bit alphanumeric string identifying a wireless network.

- **Encoding Technologies**:
  - **OFDM**: Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing, encoding data on multiple carrier frequencies.
  - **MIMO-OFDM**: Used in 4G and 5G networks for improved data transmission.
  - **Direct Sequence Spread Spectrum**: Uses pseudo-random noise to spread the signal.
  - **Frequency Hopping Spread Spectrum**: Switches carrier frequencies to spread the signal.

- **Standards and Technologies**:
  - **IEEE 802.11**: The working group responsible for Wi-Fi standards.
  - For detailed information on Wi-Fi technologies, refer to the Wikipedia page on IEEE 802.11.
  - **IEEE 802.15**: Includes Bluetooth (802.15.1) for personal area networks and ZigBee (802.15.4) for IoT devices.
  - **IEEE 802.16**: WiMAX, a wireless technology for wide-area networks with ranges up to 6 miles.

- **Wireless Network Devices**:
  - Includes personal computers, video game consoles, smartphones, tablets, and desktops with Wi-Fi cards.

- **Network Extensions**:
  - Use multiple access points to extend coverage in buildings.
  - Connect local area networks or use hotspots for extended wireless connectivity.
