1. **Overview of Mobile Attack Countermeasures:**
   - **Objective:** To prevent attacks on mobile devices both at home and in organizational settings.
   - **Concept of Controls:** Controls are mechanisms that prevent unauthorized access to resources, similar to how locks prevent access to rooms.

2. **OWASP Top 10 Mobile Controls:**
   - **Sensitive Data Protection:** Implement controls to safeguard sensitive data on mobile devices.
   - **IAM and AAA:** Use Identity and Access Management (IAM) with Authentication, Authorization, and Accounting (AAA) to secure access and manage sessions.
   - **API Protection:** Ensure APIs are secure and integrate third-party services safely.
   - **Data Storage and Application Control:** Securely manage data stored on devices and ensure organizational apps require proper authentication.

3. **General Guidelines for Mobile Platform Security:**
   - **Application Management:** Avoid overloading devices with too many apps and manage storage carefully.
   - **Trusted Sources:** Install applications only from trusted sources like the Play Store or Apple App Store.
   - **Data Deletion:** Properly delete data when disposing of devices.
   - **GPS, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth:** Turn off GPS, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth when not in use to avoid unnecessary vulnerabilities.
   - **Avoid Throughput:** Prevent your device from acting as a conduit for traffic between open Wi-Fi networks and other connections.

4. **Security Tools:**
   - **Malwarebytes:** Effective for mobile security.
   - **Personal Lockout:** Provides additional security features.
   - **Zimperium ZIPS:** Offers intrusion prevention.
   - **BullGuard Mobile Security:** Currently being tested with positive results.
   - **Norton Security:** Available for iOS devices.
   - **Comodo:** Offers strong security features for mobile devices.
