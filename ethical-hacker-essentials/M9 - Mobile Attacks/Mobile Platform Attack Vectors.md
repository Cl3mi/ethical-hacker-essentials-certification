1. **Malware and Rooting:**
   - Attackers may install malware to gain control over a device.
   - Rooting (or jailbreaking) gives attackers full control, making the device more vulnerable.

2. **Viruses and App Modification:**
   - Viruses and modifications can alter the phone’s operating system or applications.
   - These modifications can tamper with data or transactions.

3. **Data Exfiltration:**
   - Attackers might steal data from the device's SD card or capture screen content through screenshots or videos.
   - Physical backups via USB connections can also lead to data theft.

4. **Data Loss:**
   - Vulnerabilities in apps or physical access to the phone can result in data loss.
   - Physical devices are easily misplaced or stolen, posing security risks if not recovered quickly.

5. **Physical Security:**
   - Phones are small and easy to lose, as illustrated by personal anecdotes.
   - Losing a phone increases the risk of unauthorized access, depending on whether it is returned or stolen.
