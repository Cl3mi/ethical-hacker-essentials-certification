
**1. Reconnaissance:**
   - **Passive Reconnaissance:** Gathering information without directly interacting with the target. This includes searching public records, news releases, LinkedIn profiles, and employee information.
   - **Active Reconnaissance:** Direct interaction with the target through phone calls, emails, or scanning ports and equipment to gather more specific information.

**2. Scanning:**
   - Using tools to scan for open ports, live hosts, and vulnerabilities.
   - Gathering details about operating systems, devices, patch levels, application versions, and uptime status.

**3. Gaining Access:**
   - Gaining access to the network and attempting to escalate privileges.
   - Techniques include password cracking, buffer overflow attacks, denial of service, and session hijacking.

**4. Maintaining Access:**
   - Setting up backdoors or rootkits to ensure ongoing access.
   - Using tools like Trojans to maintain control over the system and manipulate data.

**5. Clearing Tracks:**
   - Hiding evidence of the attack by deleting logs or replacing them with fake ones.
   - Ensuring that their activities remain undetected and avoiding suspicion.

### Key Takeaways:
- **Reconnaissance:** Information gathering phase, both passive and active.
- **Scanning:** Identifying vulnerabilities and gathering system details.
- **Gaining Access:** Exploiting vulnerabilities to enter the network and escalate privileges.
- **Maintaining Access:** Setting up methods to ensure continued access and control.
- **Clearing Tracks:** Hiding evidence and avoiding detection.
